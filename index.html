Lokpra Language Dictionary & Reference

Lokpra is a general-purpose language constructed using a limited alphabet: p, r, l, k, o, e, with i and a used rarely. It follows a Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) word order and uses an analytic grammar style, relying on word order and particles rather than extensive inflections. Semantic roots are associated with consonants, and vowels primarily modify grammatical function and word type.

VII. Particles & Grammar

Grammatical particles and explanations of Lokpra grammar.

Particles & Grammar
Word/Particle Meaning Example Sentence
ki- past tense prefix Po ki-re ko. (I ate food.)
-a future tense suffix Pe re-a ko. (You will eat food.)
-i plural suffix Pi rek lir-i. (They see trees.)
ne not/no Po ne re. (I do not eat.)
ka question marker Pe ro ka? (Will you go?)
lo at/in/on (place) Porek relk poler-lo. (Student learns at teacher-place.)
ro to/towards Po ro ro lok. (I go to city.)
re from/away from Po rel re lok. (I come from city.)
pe be/is (copula) Pal pe pro. (Person is good.)
ler and (conjunction) Po re ko ler lek. (I eat food and water.)
ker or (conjunction) Pe re ko ker lir? (Do you eat food or plants?)
repor with (accompaniment) Po ro lo repor pel. (I go to land with friend.)
prelo because/since Po ne re ko prelo prepe po. (I don’t eat food because I am sick.)
prope therefore/so Po rek ko pro prope po re ko. (I see food is good, therefore I eat food.)
roka if (conditional) Roka pe ro, po ro. (If you go, I go.)
reka when (time) Reka ler pro le, lir le. (When sun is bright, plants grow.)
repo for (purpose) Po rak ko repo pe. (I make food for you.)
replo about/regarding Po rera replo lor. (I think about mountain.)
proka very (intensifier - rare a) Klo proka lor. (Very big mountain.)
preka little (diminisher - rare a) Kle preka ko. (Very little food.)
